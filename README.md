# GLUESTICK project page

Static site for **Don't Run with Scissors: Pruning Breaks VLA Models but They Can Be Recovered** (CoRL 2026, arXiv 2510.08464). No build step: open `index.html` or serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`).

## Layout

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The page, in paper order: hero (3D animation of Figure 3), a pinned "pruning collapse" scene (the dense 16×16 layer is pruned to 2:4 as you scroll while a Table 1 chart drops from the dense values to the Magnitude and Wanda values), then the overview as two full-screen pages: Figure 1 rebuilt in HTML under the statement "What that degradation means for a robot", where the pruned (purple) paths draw and a short failure statement ("Colliding with an object." / "Dropping a bowl.") appears under each task as its X pops; then GLUESTICK is introduced ("We propose GLUESTICK." with its symbol, then four short facts: post-pruning, training-free, pruning-algorithm agnostic, largely retains memory and speed benefits) and only after that do the orange paths draw, pulse twice and warm the stage, then the video demo "Take a look." (as you scroll from the Figure 1 page the gluestick icon flies into a dashed slot attached to the first GLUESTICK clip’s frame (each GLUESTICK clip has one); it takes about a second to be accepted (`data-state="landing"`), then GLUESTICK is applied: both slots turn orange, each GLUESTICK frame pulses orange, the GLUESTICK clips light up with their outcome lines ("Reaches the other end safely." / "Places the bowl on the cabinet."; the pruned clips carry the Figure 1 failures) and each pruned / GLUESTICK pair restarts together, a clip that ends first holding its last frame until its partner ends, with a thin progress bar along the bottom of every clip; scrolling back un-applies; a slim rail at the right edge fills while the stage holds so scrolling visibly does something; phones show everything applied with no flight), then "How GLUESTICK works." (the gluestick flies from the demo slot into this heading; pinned: a wide 3D stage runs the whole pipeline, dense layer, 2:4 pruning, gap matrix, truncated SVD, inference, as you scroll, with a boxed Offline/Online tag in its corner and the current stage’s equation in a bar along its bottom (MathJax); a five-step rail fills along, purple for the pruning steps and orange for the correction steps, and a card shows a split-flap tile that flips to the step number next to one concise statement per step; the page scrolls slowly (520vh track; per-page heights use compound selectors like `.scene-scroll.how-scroll` so the generic `.scene-scroll` height does not override them); closing card: needs only the dense and pruned models, one-time offline cost, no additional training), then "On a real robot." (Unitree G1, GR00T N1.7, Magnitude 50% 2:4: `resources/g1_pruned.mp4` and `resources/g1_gluestick.mp4`, transcoded from the authors’ .mov files to 720p H.264; both start together when in view; on the pruned clip a self-collision marker shows at 0:08 and a containment-breach marker, with everything outside the table washed magenta, from 0:44; each logs into a safety monitor under the video, and each card shows its episode outcome when its clip ends. Event times and on-video positions are set in `EVENTS` in `main.js` and in the two SVG overlays in `index.html`), then a tables page ("It holds up across sparsities, pruning methods and models.": Tables 8, 9 and 10 of the paper as three cards rising in with scroll) and a closing page with the arXiv link. The former white sections (abstract, problem, method, results, hardware, analysis, citation, footer) were removed from the page on 2026-11-09 and kept in `archive/legacy-sections.html`; their CSS and guarded JS remain, so a block can be pasted back. |
| `static/css/site.css` | Styles. White page with a black hero and 3D stage; Source Serif 4 for display headings, Inter for text and labels, JetBrains Mono for code only. While choosing a sans, `?font=plex`, `?font=dm` or `?font=source` on the page URL previews IBM Plex Sans, DM Sans or the old Source Sans 3 (the switch lives at the top of `main.js`; remove it once decided). Orange = GLUESTICK, purple = pruned. |
| `static/js/method3d.js` | Three.js scene of the GLUESTICK pipeline (stack of layers → dense 16×16 layer → 2:4 pruning → gap → truncated SVD with rank 2 → inference). The SVD stage draws the geometry of the SVD: the gap’s weights gather on a unit sphere, the gap matrix stretches it into an ellipsoid whose principal axes are the singular directions (the two long axes orange, the short one grey), the short axis collapses as the tail is discarded (the ellipsoid flattens to the rank-r ellipse), and only then do the kept directions form A and Bᵀ. Used in the hero and the method section. Append `?pruner=scissors` to the page URL to preview the scissors cutter instead of the blade; make it the default by passing `pruner: 'scissors'` in `main.js`. |
| `static/js/spotlight.js`, `static/js/spotlight-paths.js` | Figure 1 in HTML: the two panel photos plus the arrow and X-marker vector paths extracted from the paper's figure PDF. The animation only reveals them in order. |
| `static/js/charts.js` | Dependency-free, responsive SVG bar charts. |
| `static/js/main.js` | Page behaviour and every chart's data. All numbers are copied from the paper; keep them in sync with the HTML tables. |

The pinned scenes scrub with scroll. Their scroll-track lengths are `.scene-scroll` (320vh), `.fig-scroll` (260vh) and `.demo-scroll` (280vh) in `site.css`: longer means slower relative to scrolling. The scrubbing is smoothed over about 150 ms by `scrubber` in `main.js`, so wheel steps read as motion rather than jumps; the collapse scene maps scroll linearly onto the first two 3D stages, while the blade crosses the layer, copies of each dense bar slide into the Magnitude and Wanda slots, turning purple and taking the pruned values; the pruned bars then pulse twice and the paragraph rises into view.

## Adding the Unitree G1 videos

The real-world section has two placeholder frames, `#g1-video-pruned` and `#g1-video-glue`, that currently show poster stills from Figure 5.

1. Put the clips in `resources/` as `g1_pruned.mp4` and `g1_gluestick.mp4` (H.264 MP4, 16:9).
2. In `index.html`, inside each placeholder `div.vmedia`, replace the `<img …>` with the commented-out `<video …>` tag that sits right above it.
3. Delete the line `<p class="caption quiet">Video to follow; stills from paper Figure 5.</p>` under the pair.

The site's JavaScript autoplays, mutes, loops and pauses off-screen videos automatically, so nothing else needs to change.
